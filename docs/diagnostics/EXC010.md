# EXC010: Callback may throw an exception its parameter contract does not allow

A lambda, anonymous method, local function, or method group is passed to a
delegate parameter annotated `[DoesNotThrow]` or `[Throws]`, and a known
exception escapes the callback outside that contract.

`[DoesNotThrow]` allows no escaping exception. `[Throws]` allows the declared
types and their derived types. Handle the exception inside the callback, or
widen the parameter contract.

The warning is limited to callbacks the analyzer can see. A field, a delegate
parameter with no contract of its own, a local with more than one assignment,
and a method with no known contract are not reported. A contract on a
parameter that is not a delegate is EXC011.

Invoking the parameter is a separate check. A `[DoesNotThrow]` parameter is a
non-throwing call. A `[Throws]` parameter is EXC003 when the caller does not
permit the declared exceptions.
